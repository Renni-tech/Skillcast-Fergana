---
name: Fergana product scope
description: User-defined product positioning and preservation rules for SkillCast Fergana.
---

SkillCast Fergana is for the Smart Education hackathon. Its primary city is Fergana City, Uzbekistan, and its scope is “Fergana City → Uzbekistan → Global.”

It should primarily feel like a Smart Education / future-skills learning platform, not a generic career-development or HR platform. Learner onboarding is essential because it creates the profile used throughout the experience. Keep the existing Future Skills Radar, My Forecast, SDG Impact, Harada-inspired roadmap, Progress, course information, and secondary employer/workforce view.

**Why:** The user explicitly defined this positioning and asked not to redesign the application or remove working features when restoring the learner journey.

**How to apply:** Reuse existing onboarding and deterministic scoring. Explain career and workforce features through learning, skill development, and education-to-career transitions rather than replacing them or inventing unsupported personalization.
